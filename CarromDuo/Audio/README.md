# Carrom Duo original audio

These tracks and effects were synthesized specifically for Carrom Duo with
`Tools/generate_audio.py`. No recordings, commercial melodies, or third-party
samples are used. The generated audio and generator are dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

- `home_music.wav`: relaxed wooden mallets, bass, and soft percussion; seamless loop.
- `match_music.wav`: a brighter, faster original arrangement; seamless loop.
- `disc_hit.wav`, `cushion_hit.wav`: short material-specific impacts.
- `coin_score.wav`: rewarding coin capture.
- `opponent_coin.wav`: a coin awarded to the other player.
- `striker_foul.wav`: falling foul cue.
- `opponent_disaster.wav`: comic falling bass when the computer fouls or gifts you a coin.
- `queen_covered.wav`: ascending queen-cover cue.
- `victory.wav`, `defeat.wav`: distinct match-ending cues.

All files are stereo, signed 16-bit PCM WAV at 22,050 Hz. Music tails wrap across
the loop boundary. Replace individual files with identically named, licensed
WAV files to change the sound without changing gameplay code.
